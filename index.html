    Ranges, taxonomy, conservation status
    GBIF — the Global Biodiversity Information Facility. Occurrence records are CC0 or CC BY according to each publishing dataset; the datasets behind each range are listed on its entry. Conservation categories are the IUCN Red List categories as republished by GBIF. A range is a density of georeferenced records aggregated to 2° cells, not an expert-drawn map. Records GBIF flags as captive or managed are left out. States are assigned by placing each sampled record inside the Natural Earth state borders.
